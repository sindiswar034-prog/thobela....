Our Legal Services

Professional legal advice, practical solutions and dedicated representation for individuals, businesses and public institutions.

Practice areas

Experienced Legal Support

Thobela Attorneys was established in 2006. Explore our legal practice areas below to learn how our team may assist with your legal needs.

Civil Litigation

Representation in civil disputes before the Magistrates’ Courts and High Courts. We assist with contractual disagreements, financial claims, property-related disputes, damages and other civil proceedings.

Our services include:
  • Contract disputes and breach of contract
  • Debt recovery and financial claims
  • Interdicts and urgent applications
  • Property and commercial disputes

Criminal Litigation

Legal representation and guidance for clients facing criminal proceedings. Our approach prioritises the protection of constitutional rights and careful preparation at each stage of a matter.

Our services include:
  • Criminal court representation
  • Advice on criminal charges
  • Bail-related legal assistance
  • Preparation and management of defence matters

Conveyancing & Property Law

Property transactions require accurate documentation and compliance with the applicable legal processes. The firm has experience with conveyancing and government housing property transfers.

Our services include:
  • Transfer of property ownership
  • Property sale agreements
  • Conveyancing documents and registration
  • Land and title-related legal matters

Notarial Services

Our qualified notarial professionals assist with documents and legal instruments that require formal notarial execution or authentication.

Our services include:
  • Preparation of notarial instruments
  • Execution of notarial agreements
  • Authentication of relevant documents
  • Advice on notarial formalities

Commercial & Corporate Law

We assist companies, entrepreneurs and organisations with contractual obligations, commercial disputes and legal issues arising in day-to-day business.

Our services include:
  • Drafting and reviewing commercial contracts
  • Partnership and shareholder agreements
  • Commercial litigation
  • Debt collection and recovery

Labour & Employment Law

Our firm advises and represents clients in employment-related disputes, including matters before the CCMA and Labour Court.

Our services include:
  • Employment-related disputes
  • CCMA proceedings
  • Labour Court matters
  • Employment agreements and disciplinary matters

Deceased Estates, Wills & Trusts

We assist individuals and families with wills, estate planning, the administration of deceased estates and trust-related legal matters, with care for the sensitivity of these processes.

Our services include:
  • Drafting and reviewing wills
  • Estate planning
  • Deceased estate administration
  • Trust-related legal advice

Road Accident Fund & Delictual Claims

The firm has experience in claims involving motor vehicle collisions, personal injury and other forms of damage. We help clients assess available legal remedies and pursue appropriate proceedings.

Our services include:
  • Road Accident Fund matters
  • Personal injury and damages claims
  • Negligence-related disputes
  • Court representation in delictual claims

Land Claims & Restitution

The firm’s profile records work involving land claims and restitution processes in Mpumalanga, including assistance to communities asserting land rights.

Our services include:
  • Land restitution advice
  • Land claims procedures
  • Community land-rights matters
  • Land-related dispute representation

Environmental Law

We assist with legal questions and disputes involving environmental legislation, land use and environmental obligations.

Our services include:
  • Environmental legal advice
  • Environmental disputes
  • Regulatory compliance issues
  • Environmental litigation

Forensic Investigations

Our company profile includes forensic investigation among our areas of practice. The nature and scope of investigation work are assessed according to the needs of each matter.

Our services include:
  • Legal assessment of investigation findings
  • Investigation-related advice
  • Legal proceedings arising from findings

Liquor Licensing

The firm’s profile records liquor licensing services undertaken in association with another law practice, including matters relevant to hospitality businesses.

Our services include:
  • Guidance on licence applications
  • Application documentation
  • Licensing requirements and disputes

Additional areas listed in the company profile include administrative law, constitutional law, competition law, divorce, immigration and refugee law, medical negligence and third-party claims. Please contact the firm to discuss your specific matter.
